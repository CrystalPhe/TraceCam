How to use TraceCam

  1. Put your phone on a stand above the paper, then tap Start camera.
  2. Tap Image… and pick a picture from Photos or Files.
  3. Drag with one finger to move; pinch to resize; twist two fingers to rotate.
  4. Set the opacity so you can see both the paper and the image.
  5. Tap Lock so the image can’t move while you draw. Tap Unlock to adjust again.
  6. Hide controls clears the screen; Show controls brings them back.

Good to know

  • The camera fills the screen and may crop its edges. The image is positioned on the screen, not calibrated to the paper – if the phone or paper moves, realign.
  • Turning the phone sideways re-crops the camera view, so realign afterwards.
  • Keep Screen Awake is best-effort: iOS can still dim or lock the screen (e.g. Low Power Mode).
  • Lock stops the image from moving. It can’t disable the iPhone’s own touch gestures.
  • Photos and camera video never leave your phone. No accounts, analytics or uploads.
  • The app works offline only after it has loaded once online over HTTPS.
